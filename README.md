# 🍲 FoodBridge — Smart Food Donation Platform

**Share Food. Reduce Waste. Feed Hope.**

FoodBridge is a web-based platform that connects food donors with NGOs, recipients, and volunteers to help reduce food waste and redistribute surplus food to people in need.

The project demonstrates how simple web technologies can support sustainability, community collaboration, and social impact.

## 🌟 Features

- **📊 Interactive Dashboard:** View donation statistics, available food, completed donations, and estimated meals shared.
- **🍱 Food Donation:** Add surplus food with quantity, category, pickup location, preparation time, deadline, and dietary information.
- **🔎 Smart Food Discovery:** Search, filter, and sort food donations.
- **🤝 Donation Claiming:** Allow recipients to claim available food and view pickup instructions.
- **🧠 Smart Match:** Recommend relevant donations using rule-based matching criteria.
- **⏰ Urgency Alerts:** Highlight donations approaching their deadlines.
- **🚚 Volunteer Management:** View pickup tasks and update their completion status.
- **🌱 Impact Tracker:** Estimate meals provided and food redistributed.
- **🛡️ Food Safety Alerts:** Highlight expiry deadlines, missing information, and allergen details.
- **📁 CSV Export:** Export donation records for reporting and analysis.
- **👥 Demo Roles:** Explore donor, recipient, volunteer, and admin interfaces.
- **💾 Data Persistence:** Save application data in browser LocalStorage.
- **📱 Responsive Design:** Use the application on desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Prerequisites

You only need a modern web browser such as Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Firefox.

## 📖 How to Use

1. Open the application in your browser.
2. Select a demonstration role, such as Donor or Recipient.
3. Add food donations using the donation form.
4. Browse available donations using search and filters.
5. Claim a suitable donation and review the pickup instructions.
6. Update the donation status as pickup and completion progress.
7. Explore the dashboard to view statistics and estimated impact.
8. Switch to Volunteer or Admin mode to explore additional functionality.

## 💡 What Makes FoodBridge Different?

FoodBridge combines food donation management with smart matching, deadline awareness, pickup coordination, and impact estimation in a single interface.

Instead of simply listing surplus food, it helps users identify relevant donations, prioritize time-sensitive listings, and track the donation process from availability to completion.

## 🌍 Social Impact

FoodBridge aims to:

- Reduce avoidable food waste.
- Connect surplus food with people who need it.
- Encourage participation from NGOs and volunteers.
- Improve visibility into donation and pickup progress.
- Promote responsible food redistribution and sustainability.

Impact metrics are estimates for demonstration purposes and should not be interpreted as verified real-world outcomes.

## ⚠️ Limitations

FoodBridge currently uses browser LocalStorage for its demonstration data. Information is stored locally in the browser and does not automatically synchronize across users or devices.

Demo role selection is not secure authentication. Food safety alerts are informational and cannot verify whether food is safe to consume. The platform is a prototype and does not independently coordinate real-world food delivery.
